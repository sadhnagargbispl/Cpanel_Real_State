📋 Project Overview

Sky Residencia Delhi is one of India's most prestigious residential township projects, strategically located on Raiwind Road — Delhi's most sought-after real estate corridor. Developed by The Sky Is Your Limit Group, this world-class development spans 500 Kanals of premium land with over 1,200 meticulously planned residential plots.

The project is designed to offer a complete modern lifestyle within a gated community, featuring wide carpeted roads, underground electricity, natural gas lines, and dedicated parks throughout. Every plot is ideally positioned to maximize greenery views and ventilation.

With flexible payment plans starting at just ₹45 Lac, Sky Residencia Delhi is ideal for both end-users and investors seeking high-growth opportunities in one of India's fastest-growing corridors.
